## 2026/06/27 
- Started with establishing some shin-to-shin contact. I see that this is usually a thing from the [defender's perspective](https://www.jiujitsubrotherhood.com/blogs/blog/positional-study-shin-to-shin-single-leg-x-ashi-garami), but in our case, the top starts with one leg outside the defender's leg and one inside, and the idea is to put constant pressure on the defender's leg that is stuck in between, by first the outside leg, and then switching over to the inside to eventually get both legs past and pass. One thing I noticed was how constant that shin-to-shin contact was by my partner when they were passing while I let it off multiple times in the same attempt.

- This led nicely into a [knee cut pass](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F1Nd4MmLuDk). Well, the nicities weren't for me. But I think this is such a fundamental technique that it is a lifelong undertaking and I haven't had enough reps in to be even talking about it. 

- Then we did some rounds where the bottom gets to choose their positon -- mainly between a single leg x or del-hiva -- and the goal for the other person is just to break free from that contact. I definitely am very narrow minded in this case, from both positions to be honest. If I'm playing bottom, then I get too bogged down trying to retain the same grip I started with and if I'm top, I'm really just flapping around hoping I'm sweaty enough to slip by. 

- And lastly we practiced something which I thought was really fun: [inverting from rev del-hiva](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vkT6Mpn5uLY). I have slightly longer legs so this is something I'm def looking to get better at.
